About BrightMinds

BrightMinds is an extra-classes and tutoring programme based in Mangaung, Bloemfontein, Free State. Our team of dedicated tutors provides academic support to learners from Grade 4 to Grade 12 across a wide range of subjects.

Our team of dedicated tutors aims to help learners build confidence, strengthen their understanding and improve their academic performance. We provide personalised attention, structured lessons, homework assistance and exam preparation in a supportive and focused learning environment.

At BrightMinds, we believe that every learner can improve when they receive the right guidance, encouragement and consistent academic support. Our goal is to help learners develop strong foundations and become more confident in their studies.

Our Services

BrightMinds Extra Classes & Tutoring provides affordable academic support for Grade 4 to Grade 12 learners in Mangaung, Bloemfontein. Our services are designed to help learners understand their subjects, prepare for assessments and develop greater confidence in their studies.

One-on-One Tutoring

Individual academic support focused on the learner's specific needs, challenges and areas for improvement.

Exam Preparation

Revision support, practice questions and study strategies to help learners prepare for tests and examinations.

Homework Assistance

Guidance with homework and difficult concepts while helping learners develop independent problem-solving skills.

Subject-Specific Support

Academic assistance across our range of subjects, including Mathematics, Physical Science, Accounting, English, Information Technology and other school subjects we offer.
